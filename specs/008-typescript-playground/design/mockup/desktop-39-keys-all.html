Keys

The editor uses VS Code's keys, for a Mac. The menu bar, the dock and the other windows keep their own shortcuts, Option+Shift with a letter, even from inside the editor.

To leave the editor, press Ctrl+Shift+M and then Tab. To move to the next error, press F8; to hear the type of a name, press Cmd+K Cmd+I.
